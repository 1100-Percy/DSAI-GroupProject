We compared four frozen sentence representations on the unchanged 563-row v3 training set: 100-dimensional skip-gram Word2Vec and FastText trained only on each outer training partition, pretrained 100-dimensional GloVe-Twitter, and frozen 768-dimensional vinai/bertweet-base. All representations used mean pooling; BERTweet averaged hidden layers 9–12 before averaging non-special, non-padding tokens, with tokenizer normalization disabled, a maximum length of 128, float32 inference, and identical model-state checksums before and after extraction. GloVe was evaluated both directly and after applying the original lemma-and-stem normalizer to every pretrained vocabulary entry, averaging vectors that mapped to the same single-word form, without changing any input Phrase. A fold-local BERTweet-PCA100 control applied PCA before the shared probe. Evaluation used the supplied grouped five-fold split plus four shuffled StratifiedGroupKFold repeats (seeds 1–4), yielding 25 outer folds. Each outer training partition used three-fold stratified grouped inner cross-validation (seed 42) to select C from [0.001, 0.003, 0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10], choosing the smallest C within strictly less than 0.001 macro-F1 of the best. StandardScaler and balanced lbfgs Logistic Regression (max_iter=5000) were shared by all embeddings; PCA and scaling were fitted separately within each inner training partition and refitted on the outer training partition. Word-vector training was not repeated within inner folds, so inner validation text was exposed during unsupervised representation training, potentially making tuning optimistic. A sparse word-unigram plus char_wb 3–5 reference followed the same nested selection, with each feature branch independently L2-normalized and no scaling. We report pooled R0 out-of-fold macro-F1, 25-fold means and sample standard deviations, per-class F1, and paired fold differences. Differences were considered distinguishable only if the absolute mean difference was at least one percentage point and the favored configuration won at least 18 of 25 folds; overlapping folds are not independent replications. No test or audit data were read. Logistic Regression served solely as a measurement probe and does not count toward the course's required analytics models.
